# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-21)

First public release, for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux.

- Reports digital glitches while you record: clicks, spikes, dropouts, clips and invalid samples, with the calibrated detector of Estavion. Drums, consonants and room noise aren't flagged.
- A status display and a lamp that stays lit until you acknowledge; an alert window on top of your DAW when AGM's window is closed or behind, on the screen you're working on, at its scaling.
- Every glitch listed with its time, timecode (your DAW's rate and start time) and bar.beat, saved with your project; copy, export as CSV; the glitches of every track with AGM.
- Monitor while recording (default), while playing or recording, or always; Low, Medium and High sensitivity; choose what to look for.
- 100% transparent: the output is the input, bit for bit, with zero latency, natively in 64-bit in 64-bit hosts.
- Windows: VST3, as a manual-install package. macOS (Apple silicon): VST3 and Audio Unit, as a disk image with an installer. Linux (x86_64): VST3 and LV2, as a package with an install script.
- Collects no data and makes no network connections.
